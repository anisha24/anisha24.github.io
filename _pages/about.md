I’m a passionate engineer and researcher working at the intersection of Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Network Automation. I currently work as a Software Engineer at Cisco where I develop intelligent systems for automated root cause analysis, issue resolution and data observability in large-scale networked environments. My focus is on building scalable, AI-powered solutions that streamline operations, accelerate troubleshooting and enable proactive decision-making.

I hold a Master’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru. As part of my master’s thesis, I focused on developing vision models for crowd behavior and flow in complex high-density crowd environments using multi-task learning and self-supervised learning techniques. Alongside my thesis, I also had the opportunity to collaborate with Cisco Research to work on a reasoning-guided iterative learning system for nuanced video perception.

As part of my bachelor’s thesis, I developed a real-time object detection and face recognition system using YOLO aimed at assisting visually impaired individuals through contextual awareness. I interned at C-DAC, where I contributed to the development of real-time automation systems for IoT-enabled smart infrastructure. Additionally, I interned at DRDO where my work cantered on real-time video analytics and surveillance applications.

My research interests include multimodal learning, video analytics and self-supervised learning. I'm driven by a mission to leverage AI in solving high-impact problems, designing intelligent systems that are robust, adaptive and aligned with real-world needs.
